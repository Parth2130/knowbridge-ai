The Cost of Turnover

The Knowledge Loss Problem

When critical team members transition, months of unwritten context, architectural choices, and pending blockers disappear.

Scattered & Forgotten Documents

Critical specs sit stranded in private PDFs, Google Docs, Slack threads, and unindexed meeting notes that incoming engineers never find.

Hidden Technical Debt & Risks

Unassigned tasks, pending secret rotations, undocumented single-points-of-failure, and missing disaster recovery runbooks cause costly outages.

Lengthy Onboarding Delays

New engineers spend up to 90 days reverse-engineering decisions and discovering blockers that could have been handed over in minutes.

Smart Synthesis

Our AI-Powered Solution

KnowBridge AI digests unstructured technical files and compiles a comprehensive, verified project transition blueprint.

Deep Document Ingestion

PyMuPDF and python-docx extract page-level text from complex PDFs, Word documents, and text files without data loss.

Dual-Mode AI Engine

Transparent architecture runs high-precision deterministic regex/NLP heuristics locally without paid keys, while optionally integrating Gemini Cloud LLM when configured.

Verifiable Evidence Linking

Every single extracted task and architectural decision links to the exact sentence quote and page number in the original document.
